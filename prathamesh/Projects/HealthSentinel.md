# HealthSentinel

AI-based community outbreak monitoring platform by [[Prathamesh Fuke]] under [[Seris Tech]].

## Stack

React, React Native, FastAPI, Firebase, XGBoost, TypeScript.

## Highlights

- Built a full-stack healthcare intelligence platform for detecting and monitoring water-borne disease outbreaks in rural communities.
- Developed 4 specialized ML prediction systems for outbreak prediction, water contamination analysis, potability assessment, and cholera risk detection.
- Designed offline-first architecture with multilingual support for Hindi, Assamese, and Bengali in low-connectivity environments.
- Implemented real-time health dashboards, geospatial outbreak visualization, Firebase OTP authentication, and WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility support.
- Created cross-platform mobile and web interfaces for ASHA workers, healthcare officials, and community volunteers.
- Semi-finalist in Smart India Hackathon 2025.

## Key Stats

- 4 specialized ML models
- Multi-platform deployment
- Offline-first architecture
